Frequently asked questions about custom development

What is the difference between a business website and a web application?

A business website presents a company, its services or content. A web application works with users, accounts, data and processes, such as a client portal, records system or internal tool. We recommend the right scope for the goal.

Who is custom software suitable for?

It is suitable for companies whose existing tool does not fit their workflow, integrations or user roles. We review the problem and available options before proposing custom development.

Do you deliver websites and web applications across Slovakia?

Yes. DevelopED is based in Košice and works remotely with companies throughout Slovakia. Discovery, design, development, testing and handover follow the agreed process.
