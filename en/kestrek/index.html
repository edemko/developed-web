Questions

Good to know.

Have another question? Get in touch ↗

Does KešTrek transfer money?

No. It records and displays financial information. Planned payments are records, not bank instructions.

Can I separate personal finances and projects?

Yes. Folders separate different parts of your budget. The app also includes a business module.

Are these real customer finances?

No. All transactions shown here are synthetic demo data.
