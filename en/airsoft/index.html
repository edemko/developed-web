What do the screenshots show?

Private saved searches and the demo account profile. No fictional offers were published for sale.

Do I need an account?

Browsing is available without an account. Messages, your listings and saved searches require sign-in.

What do I need to publish a listing?

A signed-in account and a verified phone number. The application guides you through the required details.
