Is this a public streaming catalog?

Mega Music plays your own music collection. Your library belongs to your account.

How much storage is included initially?

Regular accounts receive 500 MB of managed storage after library activation.

Can I listen in a browser?

Yes. The web player is available after signing in to Mega Music.
