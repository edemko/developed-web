What you can do

Made for everyday use.

Practical features that work together.

01

Five-band equalizer

Shape bass, mids and treble with five bands from 60 Hz to 14 kHz. Fine-tune each band in half-decibel steps, or return to a flat sound.

02

Tags and categories

Give each song a category and multiple tags. Label moods, genres or occasions, then find the right track without remembering its filename.

03

Virtual folders

Create nested folders and rename or move songs in your managed library. Organize your collection without re-uploading audio files.

04

Play collections together

Select several folders and combine their tracks into one playback queue. Build a listening session for work, a road trip or a whole evening.

05

Advanced search

Combine alternative search terms with Enter, require exact tags and narrow by category. Play all matches or discover only the songs you have not rated yet.

06

Half-star ratings and favorites

Rate songs from half a star to five stars, even from the queue. Managed-library ratings sync across devices; hearts keep a separate favorites list in this browser for your account.

Questions

Good to know.

Have another question? Get in touch ↗

Is this a public streaming catalog?

Mega Music plays your own music collection. Your library belongs to your account.

How much storage is included initially?

Regular accounts receive 500 MB of managed storage after library activation.

Can I listen in a browser?

Yes. The web player is available after signing in to Mega Music.
